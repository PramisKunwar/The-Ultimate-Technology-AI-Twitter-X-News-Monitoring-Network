# **The Ultimate Technology & AI Twitter/X News-Monitoring Network**

**Research edition · November 2026**

For a Technology and AI news channel, the most effective X monitoring network is not simply a list of popular accounts. It should combine primary sources, executives who announce developments, researchers who discover breakthroughs, journalists with insider access, developers who test new technology, and investors who spot emerging trends.

I've organized this directory into 12 categories, prioritizing authoritative sources and useful early signals.

## **How to use this directory**

- **Primary sources:** Official company accounts, research labs, and executives. These are the best sources for confirmed announcements.
- **Breaking-news sources:** Journalists, insiders, and technology news organizations. These can help you discover stories before they become widely covered.
- **Technical sources:** Researchers, engineers, and open-source developers. These are essential for spotting genuine breakthroughs rather than marketing claims.
- **Trend sources:** Investors, founders, and emerging technology specialists. These help identify stories that may become important.

**Verification standard**

This is a curated research directory, not a claim that every profile has undergone a live, individual X verification check. Handles are supplied based on established public account references and known profiles. I distinguish established identities from accounts whose current status should be checked before inclusion in your permanent monitoring system.

A direct profile URL is provided for every account. A blue checkmark on X should not, by itself, be treated as proof of authenticity.

Sources used to cross-reference the directory include AI researcher and influencer directories, current X-focused AI account roundups, institutional websites, and Hugging Face's organization directory.&#x20;
